Security Risk Assessment & USB Exception Review

Risk Analysis | Data Protection | Security Controls

Project Overview

This case study presents an approach to evaluating security risks associated with business technology requests, including requests involving removable storage devices. The goal is to balance legitimate operational needs with appropriate protection of sensitive information.

Problem

Technology exceptions may introduce risks when sensitive information is transferred between systems or stored on removable media. Requests need sufficient information to assess the business purpose, data involved, transfer path, device ownership, and proposed safeguards.

Objectives

  • Establish the business justification for an exception.
  • Identify the sensitivity of the data involved.
  • Understand where data originates and where it will go.
  • Identify access requirements and potential exposure.
  • Document risks, mitigations, and outstanding questions.

Assessment Methodology

  1. Review the request and identify its stated business purpose.
  2. Identify the systems, devices, users, and data involved.
  3. Map the proposed data transfer path.
  4. Evaluate risks such as unauthorized access, data loss, and malware introduction.
  5. Identify applicable safeguards and additional information needed.
  6. Document findings and refer the request for the appropriate security review and decision.

Risk Evaluation Criteria

  • Data sensitivity: Determine whether personal, health, confidential, or other regulated information is involved.
  • Device security: Consider ownership, authorization, and device protections.
  • Transfer security: Evaluate how information moves between systems.
  • Access control: Identify who can access the data and device.
  • Mitigation: Consider approved alternatives and appropriate safeguards.

Deliverables

  • Structured exception review checklist.
  • Documented risk considerations and mitigation options.
  • Record of missing information and follow-up requirements.

Lessons Learned

Effective risk assessment requires more than a business justification. Understanding the data, transfer process, access requirements, and available safeguards helps ensure that decisions are based on a sufficiently complete picture of the proposed activity.

This case study describes a generalized assessment methodology. It does not disclose internal requests, device identifiers, or confidential organizational security procedures.
