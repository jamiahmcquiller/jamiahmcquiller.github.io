Evidence, Scope & Limitations

My portfolio draws on documented university cybersecurity coursework and completed TryHackMe rooms. The coursework covers network scanning, packet analysis, vulnerability assessment, firewall administration, intrusion detection, and system hardening.

Examples include the Wireshark exercise involving the telnet.data display filter and packet export, the Nmap and Masscan scanning lab, and the iptables rule-management exercises. These examples demonstrate the technical topics addressed in my coursework.

The available documents do not independently verify every command execution, scan result, screenshot, or exported artifact. Accordingly, I have not included unsupported claims about specific open ports, vulnerability totals, successful exploitation, or intrusion-detection results.

I use these labs to develop my ability to interpret technical evidence, understand security weaknesses, and connect technical concepts to defensive practices. Any future project findings will be presented with relevant evidence and clearly identified as results from the applicable lab environment.

Public portfolio material excludes credentials, sensitive configuration details, private network information, and unnecessary copies of complete course submissions.
