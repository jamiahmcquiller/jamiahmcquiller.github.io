## About Me

I am a cybersecurity and IT professional interested in vulnerability
management, endpoint security, security operations, risk assessment, and
security governance. My experience combines hands-on IT support with
security-focused work, including vulnerability remediation, patch
management, security documentation, and risk reviews.

I earned a **B.S. in Cybersecurity with a minor in Computer Science**
from the University of Michigan--Flint and hold the **CompTIA
Security+** certification. My portfolio is intended to demonstrate my
skills, professional development, and selected projects.

## Areas of Focus

-   Security controls and audit readiness
-   Identity and access management

## Featured Experience

In my current IT role, I have contributed to vulnerability remediation
efforts across **1,392 endpoints**, helping reduce identified
