Professional Background

My professional experience has allowed me to work across several areas of information technology, including enterprise endpoint management, identity and access management, technical support, cloud technologies, and security operations.

In my current role as an IT End User Computing Technician with Genesee County (Mar 2026 – Present), I contribute to vulnerability remediation, patch management, security documentation, risk assessment activities, and security control validation. I also work with security exception reviews and support compliance and audit-readiness efforts. These responsibilities have strengthened my ability to evaluate technical issues, document security considerations, coordinate remediation activities, and develop processes that support consistent security practices.

Previously, I worked in IT at Nexteer Automotive (Aug 2023 – Aug 2024), where I gained experience with identity and access management, enterprise endpoint deployment, incident triage, ServiceNow, and Microsoft 365 technologies. At Best Buy (Oct 2024 – Dec 2024), I worked as a Consultant Agent, diagnosing and resolving hardware, software, operating system, and connectivity issues across laptops, desktops, and mobile devices in a customer-facing technical environment.

I also participated in the Google Cloud Career Jumpstart Program (Aug 2022 – Dec 2022), where I gained hands-on exposure to cloud resources, access controls, cloud application deployment, and infrastructure security concepts.
